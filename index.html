WHAT CHANGED?

V27 stored.
V28 reads.

There is no label encoded into the reader. Two subthreshold native inputs meet at its existing setpoint_mod port. The original nonlinear loop makes a beat four or five ticks after their coincidence.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE?

Still an experiment.
Not a learning neuron.

The ring and the two read weights are hand-selected. Clock phases are labeled by the external measuring instrument. There is no adaptive routing, no noisy-world demonstration, and no associative retrieval. One-way wiring makes the read non-destructive by construction.

THE ANCESTORS

Old ideas, one real test.

V24's addressable reading, V9's direction-sensitive phase interpretation, and V26's native setpoint excitation suggested this experiment. Only the actual V27 core was carried into V28; the other architectures are not silently claimed as implemented.
